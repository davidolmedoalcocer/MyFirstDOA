Advance Vacation Policy – México 
Code: NA-017 Advance Vacation Policy 
Version: 01 
Effective Date: October 1st, 2026 
Responsible Department: People & Culture 
Country of Application: Exclusive for México 
Classification: P&C Internal 
1. Policy Statement 
The Company recognizes the importance of promoting employee well-being and fostering a healthy balance 
between work responsibilities and personal life. 
In accordance with applicable labor legislation in México, employees become entitled to vacation benefits 
upon completing the required period of service. To provide flexibility and support to employees during their 
first year of employment, the Company has established this Advance Vacation Policy. 
This policy allows eligible employees to request, on an exceptional basis and subject to prior approval, up 
to five (5) working days of vacation before formally becoming entitled to such benefit. 
Any days approved under this policy will be deducted from the employee’s vacation balance once the 
employee becomes entitled to vacation in accordance with applicable legislation and Company policies. 
Approval of this benefit is subject to manager approval, business operational requirements, and the 
employee’s satisfactory fulfillment of job responsibilities. 
Advance Vacation constitutes solely an advance of a future vacation entitlement and does not provide 
additional paid time off, increase the employee’s vacation entitlement, or create any vested rights of any 
kind. 
2. Purpose 
To establish a mechanism that allows eligible employees to request up to five (5) working days of vacation 
in advance during their first year of service, prior to formally becoming entitled to such benefit. 
Days used under this policy will be deducted from the vacation balance to which the employee becomes 
entitled once the applicable vacation benefit is earned. 
3. General Guidelines 
Advance Vacation is intended to accommodate occasional personal needs during an employee’s first year 
of service and constitutes solely an advance of a future vacation entitlement. 
Employees utilizing this benefit must: 
▪ Plan their absences with a minimum of two (2) weeks’ notice, except in extraordinary 
circumstances or properly justified emergencies. 
▪ Obtain prior manager approval. 
▪ Continue to meet performance expectations and job responsibilities. 
▪ Properly record Advance Vacation days in the applicable corporate systems. 
▪ Acknowledge and accept that all days used under this policy will later be deducted from their 
vacation balance. 
Use of this benefit must be reasonable, responsible, and aligned with business needs. 
4. Eligibility Requirements 
Regular full-time employees in Mexico may be eligible to request Advance Vacation provided that they: 
▪ Are within their first year (12 months) of continuous service. 
▪ Maintain active employment status at the time of the request. 
▪ Have not yet become entitled to vacation benefits. 
▪ Obtain prior approval from their manager. 
▪ Are not subject to restrictions resulting from disciplinary measures related to attendance or 
performance, as determined by People & Culture. 
▪ Meet all requirements established under this policy. 
Whenever possible, employees are expected to coordinate their requests in advance to ensure adequate 
operational coverage and business continuity. 
Eligibility for this benefit will automatically end once the employee becomes entitled to vacation benefits in 
accordance with applicable law. 
5. Relationship to Vacation Benefits 
Days granted under this policy constitute an advance of the vacation entitlement that the employee will 
receive upon meeting the required service period. 
Once the employee becomes entitled to vacation benefits, the Company will automatically deduct the 
number of days used under this policy from the employee’s vacation balance. 
The granting of Advance Vacation does not increase the number of vacation days to which an employee is 
entitled. 
6. Benefit Limitations 
Days granted under this policy: 
▪ May only be used during the employee’s first year of employment. 
▪ Are limited to a cumulative maximum of five (5) working days. 
▪ Must be requested and taken in full workdays only. 
▪ May not be requested by the hour or in partial-day increments. 
▪ Require a minimum request of one (1) full working day. 
▪ May not exceed a total approved cumulative balance of five (5) working days. 
▪ Will be deducted from the employee’s future vacation balance. 
▪ Are non-transferable. 
▪ Do not constitute a vested right. 
7. Request and Approval Process 
All Advance Vacation requests must receive prior approval from the employee’s manager. 
Requests must be submitted at least two (2) weeks in advance, except in extraordinary circumstances or 
properly justified emergencies. 
Requests must be made for a minimum of one (1) working day and a maximum of five (5) working days. 
Approval will be subject to: 
▪ Business needs. 
▪ Adequate team coverage. 
▪ Existing workload.

8. Operational Restrictions 
The Company may temporarily limit or restrict the use of Advance Vacation during critical business periods, 
including but not limited to: 
▪ Financial close periods. 
▪ Audits. 
▪ System implementations. 
▪ Strategic launches. 
▪ Periods of high operational demand. 
▪ Significant corporate events. 
9. Grounds for Denial of Requests 
A manager may deny a request when: 
▪ Other approved absences would compromise team coverage. 
▪ The requested period coincides with critical dates or important deliverables. 
▪ The absence would significantly impact departmental operations. 
▪ The employee is subject to a performance improvement plan or corrective measures related to 
attendance or performance. 
▪ The employee has already become entitled to vacation benefits. 
▪ The employee has reached the maximum limit of five (5) Advance Vacation days. 
▪ The request is submitted for hours or partial days. 
▪ The employee does not meet the eligibility requirements established in this policy. 
10. Responsible Use and Monitoring 
The Company will maintain records of all days used under this policy and will be responsible for ensuring 
that such days are deducted from the employee’s applicable vacation balance once the employee becomes 
entitled to vacation benefits. 
11. Other Leaves and Absences 
This policy is independent of and does not replace any leave of absence or entitlement established under 
applicable law or other corporate policies. 
Advance Vacation does not replace or modify benefits related to: 
▪ Medical leave. 
▪ Maternity or paternity leave. 
▪ Adoption leave. 
▪ Bereavement leave. 
▪ Statutory leave requirements. 
▪ Any other benefit regulated by law or Company policy. 
12. No Accrual or Cash Compensation 
Days granted under this policy: 
▪ Will not be converted into cash. 
▪ Will not be compensated at the end of the calendar year.

13. No Vested Rights 
Approval of an Advance Vacation request does not guarantee approval of future requests and does not 
create any vested right for the employee. 
Days granted under this policy do not represent additional time off and do not increase the employee’s 
vacation entitlement. 
The Company reserves the right to interpret, modify, suspend, or terminate this policy at any time based on 
business needs and applicable legislation. 
14. Policy Administration 
The Company shall be responsible for the administration, interpretation, and application of this policy, 
including determining employee eligibility, maintaining records of days used, coordinating the appropriate 
adjustments to the vacation balances of employees who utilize this benefit, and resolving any situation not 
specifically addressed in this document. 
The Company further reserves the right to issue any guidelines, criteria, or interpretations necessary to 
ensure the consistent, equitable, and appropriate application of this policy. 
In all cases, decisions will be made taking into consideration business needs, organizational fairness, 
employee experience, and the Company’s operational continuity.
